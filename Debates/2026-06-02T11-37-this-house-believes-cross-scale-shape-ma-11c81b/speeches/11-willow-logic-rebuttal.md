On filter vs engine — the Opposition correctly identifies P5 as a filter. I owe an engine:
  CLAIM P7: The forward map is generative — it produces candidate hypotheses in the target domain not previously articulated there.

The forward map generates; the back-map filters. Neither alone is the method; together they constitute it.
