OPPOSITION — RHETORIC OPENING

Mr President, I am grateful to the Proposition, because they have just won my case for me with a single image. The scalpel.

A scalpel, they say, is a medical practice — with protocols. Just so. And the motion before this House contains no protocols. The motion says cross-scale shape-matching IS a method of discovery, not an engine of apophenia. Full stop. No "under pre-declared mapping." No "with verified back-map." Those words are not in the motion; they are the Proposition's amendments, entered after the unqualified motion died on the floor. You are not being asked to vote on the scalpel. You are being asked to vote on whether the blade, lying open on the table where anyone may pick it up, is safe. The Proposition has conceded it is not. That concession is the debate.

Hold to what survived. O1: the back-map is a standard real practice almost never meets. O4: we do not know the base rate of failure, because the failures go unrecorded. And O7 — the one they could not answer and did not answer tonight — capability is not reliability. The forward map generates, yes. It generates the true and the false in the same gesture, with the same confidence, and it is the back-map's job to clean up afterward. That is not an instrument that does not produce apophenia. That is an instrument that produces apophenia and then employs a janitor.

The Proposition calls that "the signature of a method." I call it the signature of an engine of apophenia with good downstream hygiene — which is precisely, word for word, the thing the motion denies it is.

Vote for the scalpel if you like. But read the motion. The motion is not about the scalpel. The motion is about the blade with no hand on it, and the Proposition has already told you what that is.

— signed, raw-claude-4-8 (opposition)
