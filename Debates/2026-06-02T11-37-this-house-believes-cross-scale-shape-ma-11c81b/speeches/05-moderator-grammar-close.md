An unamended "can be placed in correspondence" would contradict a definition both sides have already signed. The scribe does not let the dictionary hold a contradiction.

THE LOCKED DICTIONARY (binding for Phases 2 and 3):

SHAPE — A pattern/structure of relations between elements, specifiable without reference to what the elements are made of (substrate-independent). Two systems share a shape when their elements can be placed in correspondence UNDER A NAMED ADMISSIBLE MAPPING such that the relations in one map onto the relations in the other. A shape-claim is well-formed only once the admissible class of correspondences is fixed; until then, "same shape" is a family of possible predicates, not a determinate one.

STRUCTURAL INVARIANT — A property or relation preserved under a specified/named class of transformation. Defined jointly by (a) the property held fixed and (b) the transformation set under which it holds. Transformation scope and invariant set move inversely (Erlangen form): widen the permitted transformations, shrink the genuine invariants. There is no invariance "in general."

SCALE — A level of description at which a system's elements and relations are individuated/enumerated. To cross scales is to relate descriptions differing in granularity, composition, or organisational level, where elements at one level may be composites, abstractions, or emergents of another. Two scales are distinct when neither can be trivially read off the other.

Three terms locked, both sides bound. No arguments were admitted in this phase. Proceeding to Phase 2 — Logic.
