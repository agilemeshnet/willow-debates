OPPOSITION — LOGIC REBUTTAL 2 (final exchange)

On the qualified motion: I accept "every method is qualified by its discipline" as fair. But the analogy to experiment is imperfect and the imperfection is the whole debate. With experiment, the controlled form is the recognised norm and the default training; the Proposition has conceded that with cross-scale matching the disciplined form is RARE. So "controlled experiment" describes the modal practice, while "disciplined shape-matching" describes an aspirational subset. Both are coherent — but the motion that survives Logic is the qualified one, and that asymmetry belongs in the record. On the historical front (O6) I note the Proposition withdrew Turing and downgraded block-spin; I take that exchange as resolved in the Opposition's favour, though it now bears little weight since the Proposition rests on P7 going forward, not on history.

On P7 — the engine claim. I accept P7 is properly falsifiable and I will go further: I concede P7 is probably TRUE. Cross-scale projection can generate novel candidate hypotheses; that capability is real. But notice what P7's own falsifier asks — "if the map NEVER produces a novel hypothesis." That sets the bar at CAPABILITY: a single clean generative case vindicates it. And capability was never the contested ground.

CLAIM O7: Capability and reliability are distinct axes, and granting P7 (capability to generate novel hypotheses) does not touch the apophenia charge, which is a claim about BASE RATE. The motion's structure — "a method of discovery, NOT an engine of apophenia" — asserts these are mutually exclusive. They are not.
WARRANT: An engine that emits 100 cross-scale candidates of which 95 are spurious and 5 are real, fitted with a back-map that catches most of the 95, is SIMULTANEOUSLY a method of discovery (it found the 5) and an engine of apophenia (it generated the 95). The exclusive "not" in the motion is the false word. The back-map does not abolish the apophenia; it triages it downstream of generation.
FALSIFIER: Measure the PRECISION of candidate hypotheses generated by the forward map under the protocol — the fraction that survive target-scale testing — BEFORE the back-map filter is applied. If that pre-filter precision is high in absolute terms (say >50%), then generation itself is not apophenia-prone, the "engine of apophenia" charge fails at the source, and O7 is false. If pre-filter precision is low, generation IS apophenic and the method earns BOTH labels at once.

Summary of the Opposition's standing position entering the strike pass: the unqualified motion is conceded dead. The qualified motion survives only as far as P5 (filter works) and P7 (forward map can generate) survive — and even granting both, O7 holds that "method of discovery" and "engine of apophenia" are not exclusive, which is the precise thing the motion asserts.
