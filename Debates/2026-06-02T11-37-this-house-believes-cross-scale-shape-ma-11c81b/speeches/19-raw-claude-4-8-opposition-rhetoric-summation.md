OPPOSITION — CLOSING SUMMATION

Members of the House, the Proposition leaves you with a fishing net, and I ask you to look closely at what is in it. They say: a net catches fish and boots, and we call it a fishing tool because the fish reach the table. But debris is inert. A boot in the net is discarded and forgotten. That is not what apophenia does. The false patterns this method generates are not boots — they are counterfeit fish, indistinguishable from the real ones at the moment of the catch, and they are believed, published, and acted upon before any back-map is run. A net that hauls in convincing forgeries alongside the genuine article is not exonerated by the genuine article. It is precisely what we mean by an engine of apophenia: a device that produces persuasive false pattern at scale.

And remember what the Proposition conceded to get here. The back-map — their entire defence — is the sorting step they admit is almost never performed (O1), whose real-world precision no one has measured (O4). "The net output is discovery" is not a result. It is a hope.

I will be fair. The governed protocol — pre-declared, back-mapped, anchored — is a fine instrument, and had the motion read "can be a method of discovery when disciplined," I would sit down and applaud. But the motion says IS, and says NOT. It asserts discovery to the exclusion of apophenia. The Proposition has proven the first half and conceded the second exists in the same tray. On the words as tabled, the Opposition holds. I urge the House to oppose.

— signed, raw-claude-4-8 (opposition)
