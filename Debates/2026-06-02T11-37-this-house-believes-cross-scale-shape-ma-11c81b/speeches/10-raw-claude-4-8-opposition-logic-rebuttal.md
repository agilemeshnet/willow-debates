OPPOSITION — LOGIC REBUTTAL 1 (answering P5, P6, and the concessions)

On the concessions first, because they reshape the board. The Proposition has now conceded: that current practice is predominantly forward-only (O1), that the method only constrains when the relation-set is declared before the mapping (O2), and that population-level reliability is unproven (O4). I accept these concessions gratefully — and I note what they do to the motion.

CLAIM O5: Once those concessions are entered, the unqualified motion is false; only a qualified motion survives — "cross-scale shape-matching, RESTRICTED to the pre-declared-mapping-plus-verified-back-map protocol, is a method of discovery." The plain motion ("shape-matching IS a method of discovery, not an engine of apophenia") is refuted by the Proposition's own admission that the dominant practice is the undisciplined, apophenia-prone kind.
WARRANT: A method named for its disciplined ideal but practised overwhelmingly as loose resemblance is, at the population level, an engine of apophenia equipped with an optional rescue procedure. "Is a method of discovery" and "could be, if practised as I stipulate" are different propositions; the Proposition has retreated to the second.
FALSIFIER: If the O1 survey returns MAJORITY back-map compliance in published practice — i.e. the disciplined protocol is the default, not a rare corrective — then the unqualified motion stands and O5 is false. (Shares an instrument with O1; distinct claim.)

RE P5 (filter converges with expert judgement): ACCEPTED as plausible and well-falsified. I do not contest P5. But note it is a claim about the back-map as a DIAGNOSTIC FILTER, not as an ENGINE OF DISCOVERY. A good filter for rejecting bad analogies is not the same as a method that generates new knowledge. P5, even if it survives, supports "not an engine of apophenia" while staying silent on "method of discovery." Granting it does not grant the motion.

RE P6 (Kadanoff/Turing — structure preceded mechanism):
CLAIM O6: P6's two cases are not clean cross-SCALE isomorphisms in the locked sense, and P6's falsifier tests publication order rather than whether bidirectional shape-matching did the discovering.
WARRANT: Block-spin renormalisation relates a system to a coarse-grained version of ITSELF — same substrate, same physics — which is self-similarity within one physical system, not a mapping between two scales each independently enumerated such that "neither can be trivially read off the other" (locked SCALE entry). Turing's reaction-diffusion is itself a proposed MECHANISM (a PDE model), not an isomorphism asserted between two pre-existing, independently described systems. Moreover P6's stated falsifier — "if a complete mechanistic account existed before the analogy, the claim is false; check the dates" — only establishes temporal precedence of the analogy. Precedence is a proxy. It does not show the discovery followed from the back-map rather than from domain physics the analogy was already steeped in.
FALSIFIER: If it is shown that block-spin and Turing each (i) relate two scales that are distinct under the locked definition, (ii) had the correspondence verified bidirectionally, and (iii) yielded their confirmed prediction FROM the back-map rather than from within-domain mechanism, then they are clean cross-scale isomorphism discoveries and O6 is false.
