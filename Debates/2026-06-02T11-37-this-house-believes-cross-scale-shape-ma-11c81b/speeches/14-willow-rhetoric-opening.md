PROPOSITION — RHETORIC OPENING

Mr President, this House does not ask you to believe that pattern-matching is magic. We buried that motion ourselves. What survives is sharper, and worth your vote.

Consider what the Opposition has not denied. They have not denied that a bidirectional back-map separates the disciplined analogy from the beautiful hallucination. They have not denied that cross-scale isomorphisms, when verified, yield predictions invisible from within a single scale. They have not denied that widening scope drives false positives toward combinatorial extinction. These are not rhetorical flourishes. They are the structural claims that walked through the Logic phase unscathed.

Now, the Opposition's strongest surviving point — O7 — says this: capability to generate does not entail reliability, and a method of discovery might simultaneously be an engine of apophenia. I want to take that seriously, because it deserves it.

A scalpel is capable of saving a life and ending one. We do not therefore refuse to call surgery a medical practice. We call it a medical practice with protocols. The qualified motion names the protocols: pre-declared mapping, verified back-map, named transformation with invariant-set. These are not ornamental. They are the difference between surgery and stabbing.

O7 asks whether discovery and apophenia can coexist in the same instrument. Of course they can — in the unqualified version, which is dead. Under qualification, the back-map functions as a filter whose outputs converge with expert judgement on which analogies mislead. Not perfect convergence. Not oracular convergence. But convergence that improves with scope, that self-corrects as you widen the net rather than narrowing it. That is the signature of a method, not a pathology.

And the forward map generates. It does not merely sort known analogies into good and bad. It produces novel, testable hypotheses in the target domain — hypotheses that were not there before the mapping was attempted. Generation under constraint is what we mean by discovery.

The unqualified motion was reckless. The qualified motion is a scalpel. This House should vote for the scalpel.

— signed, willow
